A BEGINNER ROADMAP

From learning to your first portfolio

  1. Choose one useful skill.
    Examples: short-form video editing, social-media content planning, product listing or basic website design.
  2. Learn the foundations for free.
    Use official tutorials, practise with sample projects and take notes in your own words.
  3. Build three sample projects.
    Create a mock product page, a seven-day content calendar and a simple campaign report using clearly labelled practice data.
  4. Ask for feedback.
    Show your work to a mentor, peer or small local business. Ask what is clear and what needs improvement.
  5. Offer a specific service honestly.
    Define what is included, the delivery date, revisions, price and payment terms before starting paid work.
  6. Track income and expenses.
    Keep records, understand applicable tax rules and never promise guaranteed results to customers.

Digital marketing starter ideas

  • Content calendar for a local shop
  • Product photography and captions
  • Basic website and contact page
  • Customer FAQ and WhatsApp quick replies
  • Simple campaign performance report

Responsible money basics

  • Track money coming in and going out.
  • Separate business costs from personal spending.
  • Compare fees before using payment services.
  • Learn about taxes and consumer responsibilities.
  • Be careful with debt, speculative investments and guaranteed-return claims.
