CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Privacy, terms
& responsible learning.

Starter policy text for the project owner to review and adapt before collecting data or accepting payments.

Privacy information

The current registration form is a browser-only demonstration. It does not save form entries to a server. If you connect WhatsApp, an external form service, analytics or a payment provider, update this notice to identify what information is collected, why it is needed, who processes it, how long it is kept and how users can request deletion.

Collect only information needed for the stated purpose. Do not ask users to send sensitive medical, financial or identity information through a general enquiry form. Obtain consent before sending promotional messages and provide a way to stop them.

Educational disclaimer

Life Launch Coach content is for general education and self-reflection. It is not medical, mental-health, legal, tax or investment advice. Health content does not diagnose or treat illness. Traditional, spiritual, astrology and gemstone content is presented as cultural or personal belief, not a scientifically established guarantee.

Products, offers and payments

Before accepting payment, clearly state the product, delivery method, total price, any applicable taxes or fees, refund/cancellation policy, support contact and limitations. The seven-day offer must show accurate start and end dates. Do not use a fake countdown or an unsupported reference price. Keep records and comply with applicable laws and platform terms.

Community conduct

No scams, spam, harassment, misleading income claims, coercive spiritual claims or promises of guaranteed health outcomes. Users should verify opportunities before paying or sharing personal information.

  • Effective date and policy update date
  • Payment provider and refund contact, once configured
  • Data-retention and deletion process for any connected form
